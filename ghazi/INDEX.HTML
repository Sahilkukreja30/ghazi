Workflow Process

Radio Shuttle Process

1
Box Unloading via Dock Levelers

Incoming boxes are carefully unloaded from trucks onto pallets using automated dock levelers, ensuring smooth, damage-free, and rapid handling of high-volume cargo.

2
Shrink Wrapping & Reach Truck Placement

The pallets are securely shrink-wrapped to maintain stability and hygiene, then precisely placed in designated staging areas using advanced reach trucks.

3
movement of goods using radio shuttle

Using a high-tech radio shuttle system, each pallet is efficiently transported within the racking structure and assigned to a specific storage location automatically.

4
Safe & Reliable Cold Storage

Goods are stored in a strictly monitored, hygienic, and temperature-controlled environment—ensuring complete product safety, quality, and 24/7 compliance.
