Our Strengths

01

Palletized Storage

Ghazi Foods utilizes high-density palletized storage systems across its Indore unit. This ensures precision cataloging, safe handling of heavy-duty cargo, and rapid automated retrieval while maximizing vertical space and floor efficiency.

02

State of the Art Reach Truck

Our facilities are powered by a premium fleet of battery-operated reach trucks and industrial forklifts. Integrated with automated dock-levelers, we guarantee swift, damage-free movement for temperature-sensitive SKUs.

03

Multi-Facility Eco Refrigerant

Using advanced R-404A eco-friendly refrigerants, our multi-chamber infrastructure maintains strict thermal integrity for varied sectors including dairy, pharmaceutical, horticulture, and processed frozen meats.

04

100% Power Backup

Zero temperature fluctuation is our core promise. Equipped with multiple high-load industrial generators, we provide total power redundancy to ensure uninterrupted refrigeration 24/7.

05

24 X 7 OPERATIONS

We possess capabilities to execute 24 X 7 warehousing and transportation operations with firm process mechanisms throughout the spectrum of business offerings.
